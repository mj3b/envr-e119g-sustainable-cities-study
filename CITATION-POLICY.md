# Cite the passage that supports the sentence

[Home](README.md) / Citation policy · [Source registry](cross-course/evidence-registry.json) · [AI use](AI-USE-LOG.md)

**Rule:** cite the source actually inspected, preserve its edition and qualifications, and identify the inference made from it. A locator is necessary; it does not prove support.

## A usable citation has four parts

| Part | Record | Example of the boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Identity | Author/issuer, title, date and registry ID | A utility statement establishes what the utility reported |
| Version | Supplied edition, draft/adopted status, retrieval or file digest | Ostrom's uploaded 2008 working paper is not silently recited as the assigned 2009 chapter |
| Locator | Inspected PDF page, checked printed page, or named transcript segment and timestamp | PDF and printed pagination may differ |
| Support | Exact proposition, qualifications, and observation/inference distinction | A recorded vote establishes authorization, not implementation |

## Evidence classes remain visible

| Class | Appropriate use | Do not convert it into… |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Assigned reading | An author's argument in the inspected text | A city-specific empirical finding |
| Instructor lecture | Instructor framing and application, with timestamp/speaker confidence | A quotation from the assigned author |
| TA guidance | Assignment guidance with locator; syllabus administration remains separately typed | A new empirical claim or silently amended syllabus |
| Peer discourse | A class contribution or research lead | Verified source evidence |
| External context | A primary decision record, measurement, study or attributed statement | Independent confirmation merely because the issuer is official |
| Our synthesis | An inference with linked dependencies and explicit limits | Source testimony or user-owned reflection |

## Reading and transcript rules

- Paraphrase without removing conditions, counterexamples or uncertainty.
- Verify direct quotations visually or against audio. OCR and ASR support retrieval; they do not certify exact words or speakers.
- Give PDF pagination for the supplied file; add printed pagination only when checked.
- Preserve uncertain edition equivalence. North's selected reprint inspection and Ostrom's working-paper/chapter difference remain open.
- Keep screenshot class-chat times distinct from capture dates. Prior assistant prose and historical opaque citation tokens are not primary evidence.

## External records and measurements

| If the sentence concerns… | Also record… |
| :--- | :--- |
| Decision-time knowledge | Event, publication and actor-access dates; proof of timely access |
| Resource use | Unit, observation period, facility/system boundary, collection method and missingness |
| Forecast or scenario | Assumptions, inputs and derivation; modeled status |
| Contested interpretation | Strongest competing account, source independence and unresolved discrepancy |

Several websites repeating one release remain one underlying evidence chain. Search failure means a record was not found within the documented scope; it does not prove that no record exists.

## Assignment citation workflow

Use APA or Chicago consistently, following `TA-03` and any current course directions. Generate each entry from the inspected source record, then check author, title, year, edition and locator manually. Include AI assistance actually used, following syllabus PDF p. 7. The [Assignment 1 evidence packet](assignments/assignment-01/EVIDENCE-PACKET.md) contains the focused source route; it is not a completed bibliography approved for submission.

Original PDFs, transcripts, screenshots, conversations and extraction caches stay in ignored `private/`. The repository is public by the owner’s October 9 authorization; follow the [publication policy](governance/PUBLICATION.md). Course-content redistribution and faculty access require attention to actual permissions; a citation does not grant a reproduction license.

## Cite a public checkpoint

When discussing this repository's method or research record, identify the author, repository title and version you inspected. A suitable attribution is: Mark Julius Banasihan, *Water, digital infrastructure and public accountability*, v0.3.0, ongoing AI-assisted research record developed for ENVR E-119g: Sustainable Cities. Include the [fixed release](https://github.com/mj3b/envr-e119g-sustainable-cities-study/releases/tag/v0.3.0) and the relevant file or section. Use the publication date shown on that release when your citation style requires it.

A citation to this checkpoint identifies the research record. For a factual or legal claim, cite the inspected original source and its locator as well. Preserve the draft's review status and the [AI contribution record](AI-USE-LOG.md); neither public availability nor versioning supplies scholarly endorsement.

# Cite what was actually inspected

Use the evidence registry ID plus an edition-specific locator. PDF page numbers refer to the uploaded file; add printed page numbers only when checked. Transcript timestamps are local to their named segment. Screenshot timestamps are the visible class-chat times, distinct from screenshot capture dates.

Paraphrases retain qualifications. Direct quotations require visual or audio verification against the original. OCR and ASR are retrieval aids and may misrecognize names, concepts, numbers, and speaker turns. Prior assistant text and opaque historical citation tokens cannot stand in for primary evidence.

Keep source class, support strength, and claim confidence separate. Administrative syllabus requirements are typed as course_administration under the guidance lane; spoken TA guidance remains distinct. An official utility statement is evidence of what the utility reported, not independent verification of physical impact.

For external case evidence record publication/retrieval dates, observation period, decision-time availability, units, boundaries, and underlying sources. Multiple reports repeating one source form one dependency chain.

Course materials remain private. Full PDFs, transcripts, screenshots, extraction caches, and raw conversations live only in ignored private/. No public license is granted to course content. Do not publish this repository or redistribute notes without reviewing the syllabus policy and permissions.

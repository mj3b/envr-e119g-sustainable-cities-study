# Public Research Checkpoint — Water, Digital Infrastructure and Accountability

This release provides a versioned public checkpoint of an ongoing research study. It includes research documentation, methods, evidence records, and a clearly labeled Assignment 2 review draft. Publication of this repository version does not establish peer review, faculty endorsement, completed source verification, or measured environmental outcomes.

## The question behind the study

How should a proposed environmental improvement be understood when technical descriptions, administrative responses and legal authorizations serve different purposes? This study examines that question through the Cerrillos Data Center proceedings in Chile. The current inquiry follows the judicial treatment of a proposed cooling redesign and the original environmental authorization. The [case guide](../../research/cerrillos/README.md) explains the bounded question and its evidence requirements.

The research is relevant to people who need to assess an infrastructure claim: residents, public officials, project teams and colleagues reviewing sustainability work. Its contribution at this stage is an inspectable research record. It keeps a projected improvement separate from an enforceable change, implementation and a measured outcome.

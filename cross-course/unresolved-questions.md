# Open questions · what would change the research?

[Course knowledge](README.md) · [Cumulative synthesis](cumulative-synthesis.md) · [Candidate selection](../cases/SELECTION-PROTOCOL.md)

An open question belongs here when its answer could change a claim, the case boundary or the decision to proceed. Source-fidelity gaps remain in the [omissions register](omissions.json).

## Empirical and interpretive questions

| Question | Why it is consequential | Next discriminating evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Which single decision has a recoverable contemporaneous record? | Determines whether the case can reconstruct knowledge at commitment rather than rely on later accounts. | Operative instrument, dated attachments and access/receipt evidence. |
| Did a rule constrain the choice, or did present conditions favor it? | Separates institutional explanation from labeling persistence. | A documented feasible alternative and the stated reason for rejecting it. |
| Which affected people or consequences are missing from the evaluation? | May change the judgment even when aggregate benefits are documented. | Disaggregated outcomes and a defensible account of representation. |
| Did a monitoring condition operate during a relevant event? | Distinguishes a declared control from observed behavior. | Trigger, observation, response and enforcement records. |
| Did the control explain the result? | A before/after pattern can reflect weather, workload or changed boundaries. | Comparable periods and evidence assessing these alternative causes. |
| What local ecological threshold makes the result adequate? | A reduction alone cannot establish maintenance of a stock or function. | Regeneration, assimilative-capacity or resource-condition evidence with uncertainty. |

## Normative questions require reasons as well as data

| Question | What evidence can clarify | What remains a judgment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Which dimensions and groups should receive weight? | Consequences, exposure, participation and stated preferences. | A defensible account of what matters and why. |
| Which losses may be compensated? | Substitutability, durability and who receives the replacement benefit. | Whether the proposed tradeoff is acceptable. |
| How should future consequences enter the decision? | Sensitivity to time horizon and discount assumptions. | The justification for the chosen treatment of future people and ecological functions. |

## Decisions deliberately left open

**Geography:** Georgia, Hawaiʻi, Memphis, Loudoun/Northern Virginia, The Dalles and Tucson/Pima remain candidates. No default selection follows from controversy, familiarity or document volume.

**Personal engagement:** Mark’s reason for caring about a bounded case must come from Mark. The repository can prepare evidence and questions without inventing a reflection.

**Fidelity:** North edition mapping, Ostrom chapter equivalence, lecture speaker verification and full coverage remain unresolved. Better formatting or additional synthesis cannot close those gaps.

**Disposition rule:** narrow the question when a smaller claim is supportable; hold when a missing record could decide it; reject an interpretation contradicted by evidence; abstain when available evidence cannot distinguish the alternatives. Record the reason instead of treating incompleteness as failure to sound certain.

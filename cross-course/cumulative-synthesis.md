## Four connected tests

| Test | Course contribution | Evidence the case must supply |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Explain the choice** | North: rules, enforcement and institutional change (`N-01`–`N-03`, PDF pp. 1, 2, 5). | Operative authority, prior commitments, alternatives and the sequence linking them to the decision. |
| **Examine the standard of success** | Sen: plural evaluation and public reasoning (`S-01`–`S-03`, PDF pp. 5–7). | Explicit criteria, affected groups, reasons for tradeoffs and disaggregated consequences. |
| **Observe the arrangement** | Ostrom: conditional cooperation and observable adherence (`O-02`, working-paper p. 25; `O-04`, p. 12). | Monitoring, relevant events, behavior and feasible corrective responses. |
| **Assess physical adequacy** | Costanza and Daly: stocks, scale and ecological capacity (`CD-01`, p. 3; `CD-02`, p. 6; `CD-04`, p. 9). | Comparable resource measurements, system boundaries and locally relevant thresholds. |

The tests can produce different answers. An institution can coordinate behavior while imposing unacceptable burdens under a stated evaluative standard. A project can reduce intensity while increasing total demand. A transparent process can still leave the physical outcome unknown. These are possibilities to examine, not conclusions about the candidate cities.

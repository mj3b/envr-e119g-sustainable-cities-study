# Course knowledge and research applications

[Home](../README.md) / Course knowledge

The first two classes establish four analytical tasks: explain institutional persistence, evaluate development, examine conditional cooperation, and specify ecological constraints. The [course-to-research bridge](course-to-research.md) makes each task operational without treating theory as evidence of a local outcome.

| Reading | Argument reconstruction | Main research use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| North | [Institutions and economic change](../modules/01-theories-sustainable-development/class-01-economic-social-development/north-1994/reading-notes.md) | Trace rules, incentives, enforcement, and switching costs |
| Sen | [Plural evaluation](../modules/01-theories-sustainable-development/class-01-economic-social-development/sen-2000/reading-notes.md) | Identify whose outcomes and values enter the assessment |
| Ostrom | [Conditional cooperation](../modules/01-theories-sustainable-development/class-02-environmental-sustainability/ostrom-2009/reading-notes.md) | Examine monitoring, reciprocity, and response in context |
| Costanza and Daly | [Natural capital and physical scale](../modules/01-theories-sustainable-development/class-02-environmental-sustainability/costanza-daly-1992/reading-notes.md) | Distinguish stocks, flows, efficiency, and ecological thresholds |

Each reading folder also contains an argument map, questions, and canonical claim references. [Class 1 lecture synthesis](../modules/01-theories-sustainable-development/class-01-economic-social-development/lecture/lecture-synthesis.md) and [Class 2 lecture synthesis](../modules/01-theories-sustainable-development/class-02-environmental-sustainability/lecture/lecture-synthesis.md) preserve the instructor and TA lanes.

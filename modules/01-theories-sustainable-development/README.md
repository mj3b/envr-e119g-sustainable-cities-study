# Module 01 · theories of sustainable development

[Course map](../../COURSE-MAP.md) · [Research method](../../METHODOLOGY.md) · [Cross-course synthesis](../../cross-course/cumulative-synthesis.md)

> **Organizing question:** How do institutions shape development choices, and what would make those choices socially and environmentally defensible?

## Two connected inquiries

| Class | Central problem | Analytical contribution | Open the dossier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 01 · Economic and social development | How choices arise and how their consequences should be judged. | North: institutions and change. Sen: plural evaluation and public reasoning. | [Class 01](class-01-economic-social-development/README.md) |
| 02 · Environmental sustainability | How cooperation and ecological conditions affect sustainable outcomes. | Ostrom: conditional cooperation. Costanza and Daly: natural capital and physical scale. | [Class 02](class-02-environmental-sustainability/README.md) |

## What each dossier contains

| Layer | Question answered | Evidence boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reading notes and argument maps | What does the inspected text claim, and what warrants the argument? | Supplied edition and located passages. |
| Lecture alignment | How does classroom framing align with, extend or qualify the reading? | Named transcript segment and timestamp; speaker inference disclosed. |
| Peer discourse | Which questions or interpretations emerged in discussion? | Evidence that a comment was made; broader claims require independent support. |
| Synthesis and applications | What could we investigate, and what would change the interpretation? | Our analytical proposals, not author findings or measured local outcomes. |

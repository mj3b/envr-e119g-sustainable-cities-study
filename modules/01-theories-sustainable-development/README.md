> **Organizing question:** How do institutions shape development choices, and what would make those choices socially and environmentally defensible?

## Two connected inquiries

| Class | Central problem | Analytical contribution | Open the dossier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## What each dossier contains

| Layer | Question answered | Evidence boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reading notes and argument maps | What does the inspected text claim, and what warrants the argument? | Supplied edition and located passages. |
| Lecture alignment | How does classroom framing align with, extend or qualify the reading? | Named transcript segment and timestamp; speaker inference disclosed. |
| Peer discourse | Which questions or interpretations emerged in discussion? | Evidence that a comment was made; broader claims require independent support. |
| Synthesis and applications | What could we investigate, and what would change the interpretation? | Our analytical proposals, not author findings or measured local outcomes. |

**Recommended route:** class overview → reading argument → lecture alignment → synthesis → unresolved evidence. The aim is to make each inference inspectable before it becomes a conclusion.

## Read the substantive dossiers

The original JSON files were small routing records under a schema permitting only source IDs, claim IDs, an argument-map path and limitations. Detailed reading review had accumulated elsewhere without updating this view. The current schema adds a structured substantive-analysis profile, and all six readings now have one. Existing claim IDs and approval gates are unchanged.

[Class 1](class-01-economic-social-development/README.md) distinguishes institutional explanation from plural evaluation. [Class 2](class-02-environmental-sustainability/README.md) distinguishes cooperation mechanisms from ecological stock/flow criteria. [Class 3](class-03-sustainable-cities/README.md) adds the sustainability boundary and document-analysis method. Short `claims.json` and `concepts.json` files remain deliberate ID indexes, not claims of comprehensive synthesis.

For Assignment 2, North and Satterthwaite provide the theoretical center. Lowe informs coding. Sen, Ostrom, and Costanza–Daly remain available for justified, bounded uses; reading coverage does not require citing every author. All substantive profiles distinguish the authors' claims from proposed Cerrillos applications and keep human review pending.

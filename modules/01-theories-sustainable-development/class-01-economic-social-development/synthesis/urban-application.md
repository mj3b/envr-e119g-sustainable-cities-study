# Class 01 · reconstruct one development decision

[Class overview](../README.md) · [Candidate discovery](../../../../cases/DISCOVERY-ASSESSMENT.md) · [Research objects](../../../../cases/RESEARCH-OBJECTS.md)

> **Design question:** What did an institution know when it committed shared resources, which alternatives were feasible, and whose concerns entered the decision?

**Status:** proposed application of `SYN-01`. This earlier design predates the [current Cerrillos selection](../../../../research/CURRENT.md). This design can be applied to one bounded decision within any viable candidate.

## Evidence packet

| Analytical task | Record to retrieve | Inference it can support |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Establish authority | Operative ordinance, order, delegation or agreement. | Which actor could approve, condition, delay or refuse the action. |
| Establish decision-time knowledge | Dated staff report, attachments and hearing materials. | Which information was available to an actor before commitment; receipt may still need proof. |
| Reconstruct alternatives | Deliberations, rejected options and cost or feasibility studies. | Why a documented alternative was excluded; silence alone proves little. |
| Examine plural evaluation | Explicit criteria, distributional analysis and responses to objections. | What counted as a benefit or burden and whose concerns were considered. |
| Trace later consequences | Comparable measurements and implementation records. | What followed the decision; causal attribution requires more evidence. |

## Competing explanations

| Explanation | Distinguishing observation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Inherited commitments restricted the choice. | A feasible alternative was rejected because of a prior commitment or enforcement constraint. |
| Present physical or economic conditions favored the choice. | The option remained preferable under credible alternatives to the inherited arrangement. |
| The evaluation omitted a consequential group or dimension. | Disaggregated evidence changes the comparison or reveals an unaddressed burden. |
| The chosen criteria were adequate for the decision. | Omitted dimensions do not materially change the result under credible uncertainty. |

## Attribution and limits

Institutional questions draw on `N-01`–`N-03` (North PDF pp. 1, 2, 5). Evaluative questions draw on `S-01`–`S-03` (Sen PDF pp. 5–7). Jurisdiction and distribution extend the lecture (`L1-04`, L1C 08:33–11:28; `L1-05`, L1E 03:56–06:36).

The packet should distinguish a public document from evidence received by the decision-maker. Later knowledge can test assumptions, but it cannot silently be inserted into the earlier decision record.

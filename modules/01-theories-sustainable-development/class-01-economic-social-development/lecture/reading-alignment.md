## A divergence worth keeping

`P1-02` (L1E 02:58–03:56) proposes exclusively bottom-up institutional change. That is a peer hypothesis. The coded North claims do not establish its exclusivity. Treating it as an author conclusion would erase both the stronger causal assertion and its uncertain support.

**Review action:** inspect the paired passages before approving the relationship. Reading–lecture agreement does not substitute for fidelity review of either source.

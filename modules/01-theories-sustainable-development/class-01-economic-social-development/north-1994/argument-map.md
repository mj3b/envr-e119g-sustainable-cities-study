## Two explanations to distinguish

| Proposed account | Evidence that would support it | Evidence that would weaken it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Inherited commitments constrain the choice. | Contemporaneous records identify sunk commitments, enforcement constraints or opposition that excludes a feasible alternative. | The alleged constraint was inapplicable, waived, or irrelevant to the choice. |
| Present technical or economic conditions explain the choice. | Comparable alternatives remain less feasible even after inherited constraints are removed. | Decision records show a feasible option was rejected chiefly to protect an inherited arrangement. |

These accounts may coexist. Explain their relative contribution without pretending that one document identifies a causal effect.

## Boundary

Explanatory success does not justify the resulting distribution of benefits and burdens. North helps frame why a choice occurred; the evaluative standard must be stated separately.

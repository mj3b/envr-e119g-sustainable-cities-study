### Worked research prompt

For one infrastructure approval, identify the actor with authority to change a condition. Compare the written discretion with what that actor actually considered. A claim that “the system made the decision inevitable” remains unproven until realistic alternatives and constraints have been examined.

## Short oral defense

Explain one claim in plain language. Identify its page, the inference you add, and the observation that would change your view. If the supplied source cannot answer a question, name the missing evidence instead of completing the argument from memory.

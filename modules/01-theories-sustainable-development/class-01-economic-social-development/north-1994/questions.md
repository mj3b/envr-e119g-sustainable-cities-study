# Reading and research questions

Answer from the source first; then identify what additional evidence a case application requires. These questions test scope and mechanism rather than recall of an author’s slogan.

1. Which formal rule, informal expectation, and enforcement practice each affect the same decision?

2. What evidence makes persistence an inherited constraint rather than the best available present choice?

3. Which organization acts within the institution, and which actor can change the rule?

4. What observable sequence would weaken an account based on switching costs?

5. How would an efficient but exclusionary institution appear under Sen’s evaluative concerns?

For discussion, choose one question, give a tentative answer, name the claim and page that support it, and state the observation that would change your answer. Keep an unresolved question unresolved when the reading cannot settle it.

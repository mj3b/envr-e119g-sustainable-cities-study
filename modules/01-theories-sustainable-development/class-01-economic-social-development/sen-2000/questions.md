### Attribution test

State what the essay says about plural evaluation before using the lecture’s capabilities framing (`L1-03`, L1E 06:36–09:50). An answer that merges the two cannot demonstrate source fidelity.

### Decision test

Specify a feasible alternative and the dimension on which it could outperform the preferred option. If there is no comparable evidence, record an open question rather than constructing a numerical ranking.

## Short oral defense

Explain one claim in plain language. Identify its page, the inference you add, and the observation that would change your view. If the supplied source cannot answer a question, name the missing evidence instead of completing the argument from memory.

## Analytical use

### 1. Keep the object larger than the metric

The inspected essay critiques narrowing evaluation to one measure (`S-01`, `S-02`). It does not make measurement useless. A project’s revenue estimate can be informative while leaving electricity access, environmental exposure or the distribution of opportunity unexamined. Those case dimensions are our proposals; their relevance must be established locally.

### 2. Make the evaluative choice explicit

Selecting outcomes is already a judgment. `S-03` makes the reasoning behind that choice part of the inquiry. Our practical adaptation is a small, decision-relevant indicator set with an explanation of whose concerns each indicator represents. Record missing groups before treating the selection as adequate.

### 3. Separate plural evaluation from an unlimited checklist

More indicators can overwhelm a decision without improving it. A defensible application asks which omitted dimension could actually change the comparison, then tests plausible tradeoffs. Sensitivity to alternative weights is a proposed method here, not a procedure attributed to the essay.

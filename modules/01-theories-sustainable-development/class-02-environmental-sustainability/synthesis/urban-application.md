# Class 02 · test a resource-use condition

[Class overview](../README.md) · [Quantitative notes](../../../../cases/QUANTITATIVE-NOTES.md) · [Worked decision example](../../../../cases/worked-example.md)

> **Design question:** Did an adopted resource-use condition operate when it mattered, and what evidence would show a consequence for the shared resource?

**Status:** our application of `SYN-02` and `SYN-03`. No selected geography or measured local effect is asserted.

## Follow the condition through four stages

| Stage | Required record | What remains unproven |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Declared | Executed requirement with a defined actor, trigger and limit. | Whether the requirement was implemented. |
| Implemented | Working monitoring equipment, reporting procedure and responsible authority. | Whether the control operated during a relevant event. |
| Operated | Dated observation, detected condition and documented response. | Whether the response materially affected the resource. |
| Effective | Comparable measurements, relevant threshold and assessment of alternative causes. | Long-run adequacy or transfer to another context. |

The stages are a repository method adaptation. Ostrom motivates inspecting observable adherence (`O-02`, working-paper p. 25). Nora’s distinction between outputs and outcomes (`L2-03`, 1:27:11–1:29:31) prevents treating an executed agreement as an environmental result.

## Worked calculation: efficiency and total demand

**Illustrative arithmetic only. These are invented values, not case measurements.** Assume the same system boundary, service definition and annual period.

| Quantity | Before | After |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Useful service units | 100 | 150 |
| Energy per service unit | 2 MWh | 1.5 MWh |
| Total energy | 200 MWh | 225 MWh |

Intensity falls by 25%; total energy rises by 12.5%. Multiplying rate by activity establishes the arithmetic. It does not establish that efficiency caused the activity increase, so this example alone cannot prove rebound. The distinction operationalizes `CD-02` (PDF p. 6).

## Rival explanation and ecological test

A post-agreement decline could reflect lower production, weather, relocation or a changed reporting boundary. `H-01` and `H-02` in the [research register](../../../../cases/research-objects.json) preserve monitoring and changed operations as competing explanations.

Even a verified reduction does not establish ecological adequacy. Apply `CD-04` (PDF p. 9 / printed p. 44) by identifying the relevant regeneration or assimilative constraint, its uncertainty and the affected period. If that threshold is unavailable, the adequacy conclusion remains open.

# Class 02 · cooperation and ecological adequacy

[Class overview](../README.md) · [Concept map](concept-map.md) · [Urban application](urban-application.md)

> **Working synthesis:** an arrangement can produce cooperation without demonstrating that its aggregate resource use is ecologically adequate.

## What each inquiry tests

| Question | Ostrom | Costanza and Daly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Why might actors cooperate? | Observable adherence, reciprocity and context (`O-02`, PDF p. 25; `O-04`, p. 12). | Not the central question in the inspected claims. |
| What must resource use respect? | Ecological and social conditions interact (`O-03`, PDF p. 26). | Stocks, functions, regeneration and assimilative capacity (`CD-01`, p. 3; `CD-04`, p. 9). |
| What could a success claim miss? | Failed monitoring, coercion, excluded actors or a change in conditions. | Absolute demand, a depleted stock or a service that compensation cannot replace. |

## Tensions to preserve

| Tension | Research implication | Serious counterargument |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Agreement and physical limits | Test the aggregate permitted use against the relevant threshold. | A well-designed agreement may incorporate the threshold; inspect it before assuming failure. |
| Local cooperation and displaced burdens | Map effects outside the participating group or jurisdiction. | Some effects may be negligible or governed elsewhere; establish magnitude and authority. |
| Efficiency and scale | Measure both intensity and total demand over a common boundary. | Efficiency can reduce totals when activity does not offset the saving. |
| Valuation and substitutability | Explain what a monetary offset is meant to preserve. | Valuation can improve visibility even if it does not settle the acceptability of loss. |

The final two tensions connect `CD-02` (PDF p. 6) and `CD-03` (p. 9) to Nora’s valuation discussion (`L2-04`, 1:22:17–1:27:11). They are analytical questions, not evidence that any named project fails.

## A research standard

A strong case account needs both a plausible behavioral mechanism and a defensible outcome measure. “The parties complied” and “the resource remained within a limit” are different propositions with different evidence requirements.

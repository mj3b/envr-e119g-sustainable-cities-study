## Implications for the research design

A proposed water project, an operating facility and a measured change in resource conditions represent different evidentiary stages. Our application of `L2-03` requires a record at each stage. `L2-02` then asks whether an apparent success depends on local conditions that another place lacks.

The selection guidance supports discovering several candidates before choosing a bounded inquiry. It does not require six full case studies. Familiarity matters because the student must recognize errors, explain uncertainty and defend a judgment without relying on an AI summary.

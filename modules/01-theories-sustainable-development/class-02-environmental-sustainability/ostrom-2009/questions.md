### Worked research prompt

For a curtailment obligation, distinguish the contract, a qualifying grid event, the operator’s response and the resulting demand change. Missing event-level evidence prevents a claim about operational effectiveness even when the agreement itself is verified.

## Short oral defense

Explain one claim in plain language. Identify its page, the inference you add, and the observation that would change your view. If the supplied source cannot answer a question, name the missing evidence instead of completing the argument from memory.

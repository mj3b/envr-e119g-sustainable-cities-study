## Rival interpretations

| Interpretation | Evidence required | Failure condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A proposed substitute preserves the ecological service. | Comparable service, relevant location and period, durability and implementation evidence. | The substituted service fails under the conditions that matter, or shifts the burden elsewhere. |
| Efficiency delivers an absolute resource reduction. | Rate and activity series using the same boundary. | Activity rises enough that total demand fails to decline. |
| Financial valuation adequately represents the decision’s consequences. | Explicit rate, horizon, distribution and treatment of irreplaceable functions. | A material loss is excluded or cannot be represented by the proposed compensation. |

These are case-level tests. The reading frames why they matter; it does not resolve them for a data center or city.

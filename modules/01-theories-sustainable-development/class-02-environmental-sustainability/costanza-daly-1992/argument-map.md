# Argument reconstruction: Costanza and Daly

1. Natural-capital stocks sustain economically and socially relevant flows (CD-01).
2. Manufactured capital often relies on those stocks as a complement (CD-02).
3. Greater efficiency does not itself establish a reduction in physical throughput (CD-02).
4. Maintaining a total monetary capital measure can therefore conceal losses relevant to sustainability (CD-03).
5. An evaluation must examine natural capital and the physical scale of resource use explicitly.

This is our reconstruction of the inspected claims. The crucial warrant is limited substitutability of the particular ecological function. An application needs evidence about that function, not only a general appeal to natural capital.

A serious alternative holds that innovation or substitution can preserve the needed service at lower resource cost. Test that claim at the relevant scale and time: is the substitute technically available, deployed, durable, and sufficient for the affected population? Also examine whether displaced impacts occur outside the chosen boundary.

The conclusion does not identify a city-specific threshold or quantify future damage. Those are empirical and normative tasks for the case. Ostrom helps investigate how collective rules might respect a constraint; cooperation alone cannot establish that the constraint is met.

Support: CD-01, CD-02, CD-03. This reconstruction is provisional and requires human review alongside the supplied source.

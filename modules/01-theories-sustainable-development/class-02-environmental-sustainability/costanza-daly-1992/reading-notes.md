# Costanza and Daly, Natural Capital and Sustainable Development

Conservation Biology 6(1), 37–46 (1992). PDF page 2 = printed 37.

CD-01: Natural capital is a stock yielding future goods and services, including ecosystem functions. (PDF page 3, printed 38; source_checked).

CD-02: Natural and manufactured capital are largely complementary; efficiency improvements differ from increased throughput. (PDF page 6, printed 41; source_checked).

CD-03: Strong sustainability maintains natural and human-made capital separately; operational principles constrain throughput by ecological capacity. (PDF page 9, printed 44; source_checked).

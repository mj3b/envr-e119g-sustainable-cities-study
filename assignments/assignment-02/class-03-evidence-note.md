## Evidence that would support an account of human activity

| Question | Evidence sought | What remains open without it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What could people know? | Dated materials, recipients, recorded explanations, requests for clarification. | A public report alone does not establish receipt or understanding. |
| What could people influence? | Consultation records, decision options, authority, and any revisions following an objection. | Participation counts do not establish influence or community consent. |
| Why could an institution act? | Relevant responsibilities, resources, staff, and a documented decision route. | A formal title does not establish practical capacity. |
| What action occurred? | A dated decision, implementation record, and subsequent observation. | Intention or authorization does not establish implementation. |
| What benefit reached households? | Appropriate service and affordability measures for a defined population and period. | A supply volume or asset completion alone leaves that result unresolved. |

These questions apply North and Ostrom to the case. Lowe's policy-content method leaves implementation and tracking for further research (`LOW22`, pp. e890-e891). Satterthwaite's criteria require attention to household needs and environmental capital beyond the approving jurisdiction and into the future (`SAT97`, pp. 1669-1670, 1680-1682). Good cooperation would still need evaluation against ecological and distributional outcomes.

## Why the AI papers are included

*Embedded Assessments for Frontier AI* proposes access to infrastructure, personnel, and documentation, and assesses the route from monitoring to review (`EXT-GOVAI-EA`, sections 3.1.1 and 3.2). The principal lesson for this case is a limit: public records cannot establish every internal practice or replace direct observation. No embedded assessment is proposed here.

Kara's *Improving Frontier AI Incident Reporting Regimes* recommends preserving actions, system versions, and human interventions, with named responsibility and follow-up on corrections (`EXT-GOVAI-IR`, recommendations 5 and 7). The adaptation is to preserve evidence provenance and a correction record for this research. These are proposed practices, not claims that the semester workflow has been validated or that frontier-AI regulation applies to it. The brief states it has not undergone official peer review.

The previous memo made two course sources explicit: Sen and the Rio study. Version 0.8 connects seven readings to distinct research and design choices. Sen remains the main evaluative principle; North explains response authority. The other readings define environmental boundaries, evidence methods and practical tests. This responds to the assignment’s learning requirement while keeping the resident-response question narrow.

## The seven selected readings

| Reading and inspected passage | What the memo takes from it | Limit of the application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Gamperl et al. (2023), publisher PDF pp. 16–18 | Test communication routes, coordination and ongoing support with intended users. | Newly retrieved version; equivalence to the earlier upload unestablished. Warning effectiveness remains unproven; no local transfer is assumed. |
| Blackman & Hoffmann (2025), pp. 515–516 | Record assistance and distinguish a bundled intervention from the effect of its individual components. | Recording assistance alone does not identify a causal effect. A volunteer usability trial is not evidence of environmental improvement. |

Full references appear in the [memo](memo.md#references). North uses the inspected EAFIT reproduction; equivalence to the syllabus AER edition remains unverified. Satterthwaite uses the supplied 1997 article; equivalence to the listed 2021 chapter remains unverified.

## Continuity and review limits

The submitted first assignment used North, Sen and Costanza–Daly to frame accountability for long-lived resource commitments. Assignment 2 brought North, Satterthwaite and Sen into a narrower court/instrument question, with Lowe as a methodological precedent and Amazonia as context. The present revision makes these foundations visible in a resident-response inquiry and brings later methodological lessons into the proposed trial. Assignment 2 v0.11 is unchanged.

Codex and collaborating agents compared the actual submitted PDFs, inventoried access, inspected the named passages and drafted the integration. These are AI-assisted source checks, not independent human review. The prior source and claim decisions retain their original scope. No fieldwork, new case label, Jev evaluation or course submission occurred.

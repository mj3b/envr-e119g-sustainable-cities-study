## What the proposed connections mean

The framework asks whether evidence supporting approval can be inspected, tested over time and connected to an institution responsible for action. The [companion’s six-link table](HUMAN-REVIEW-APPENDIX.md) specifies the records needed for each question. The links need not form a rigid linear process or sit within one institution.

Evidence that all six links exist would still leave open whether the allocation is fair, ecological conditions support it, and any safeguard caused an improvement. Missing records also leave open whether an informal or differently documented safeguard operated. These distinctions preserve the strongest favorable interpretation and allow rejection or revision of the framework itself.

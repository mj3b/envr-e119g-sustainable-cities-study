# Methodological influences and limits

These influences inform how the repository asks questions and evaluates evidence. The operational rules are our adaptation, not an endorsement by the organizations or proof that a method has been validated for environmental governance. Official pages were inspected during the September 2026 audit.

| Influence | Source and relevant idea | Repository application | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E5 | Four private writing references identified in WRITING.md | Audience, argument, evidence, voice, and disciplined revision | A writing system cannot establish empirical truth |
| LessWrong | [About](https://www.lesswrong.com/about): improving reasoning and decision-making | Expose assumptions, compare explanations, state update conditions | No universal LessWrong prose rulebook is claimed |
| The Decision Lab | [How we work](https://thedecisionlab.com/how-we-work): behavioral research and experimentation | Examine actors' incentives, context, feasibility, and observable behavior | Organizational self-description is not independent evidence of intervention effectiveness |
| METR | [Research organization](https://metr.org/about) and [2025 developer study](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) | Distinguish expectations from measured performance; define the task, population, comparison, and limits | Results about developers do not establish environmental outcomes |
| Digital Emissions / IRIS | DE-PROV and DE-BOUND private snapshots | Trace source → assertion → transformation → finding → decision; check time and facility identity | Method documentation does not supply metered resource use |
| Green Software Foundation | [SCI specification, version 1.1.0](https://sci.greensoftware.foundation/) | Declare system boundary, functional unit, energy and carbon inputs, embodied allocation, and method | A software carbon rate does not settle water, equity, or city sustainability |
| Node & Norm / GDI | NN-CHARTER and NN-GDI snapshots | Reconstruct authority, alternatives, evidence available then, and correction | A complete decision record does not prove effective human judgment or a good decision |

SCI expresses carbon emissions per functional unit, with operational and embodied components. Applying it requires an explicit boundary and comparable measurement or modeling. Repository comparisons must also disclose total activity and absolute impacts. An intensity improvement and a rise in total impact can coexist. That arithmetic alone does not show that efficiency caused demand growth.

The four user-supplied research-skills screenshots support reflection, evaluation, visualization, and plot interpretation. Their original authorship is unknown. Their machine-learning examples are adapted in [research skills](../study/research-skills.md); they are neither additional course requirements nor validated urban research instruments.

No affiliation, certification, or methodological authority should be inferred merely from including these influences. Mark's own named credentials require confirmation before use in deliverables.

# How this research earns its conclusions

[Home](README.md) / Methodology · [Worked design](cases/RESEARCH-DESIGN.md) · [AI assurance](governance/README.md)

> **Working design:** comparative discovery leading to one bounded urban decision case. Course interpretation and empirical case research have separate evidence requirements. Geography remains open.

The central question is what an institution knew when it committed shared resources, how authority and uncertainty shaped that decision, and what later evidence can establish about its consequences. Current material supports documentary reconstruction and research design. It does not yet support an estimate of environmental impact caused by governance.

## Two connected lines of inquiry

| Line | Unit of analysis | Product | Test of adequacy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Understand the course | An author's argument and an instructor's treatment | Reading dossier → lecture comparison → qualified synthesis | Can a reader reconstruct the argument and locate its qualifications? |
| Investigate a case | One action by a named authority in a place and period | Decision record → mechanism test → bounded finding | Can evidence distinguish the explanation from a credible rival? |

Course theory determines useful questions. It does not supply missing city observations. The [course-to-research matrix](cross-course/course-to-research.md) makes that transition explicit.

## The working sequence

| Step | Inspectable output | Advance when… |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frame** | Question, population, outcome, period, and descriptive/causal/normative scope | Empirical propositions can face contrary evidence; normative judgments expose values and contestable reasons |
| **Discover** | Six-candidate [assessment](cases/DISCOVERY-ASSESSMENT.md), search record, missingness | Candidates receive a comparable source pass; selection rationale is written |
| **Reconstruct** | Claims with locators; dated actors, authority, alternatives and evidence access | Facts available then are separated from later knowledge |
| **Translate** | A bounded task, permitted inputs, expected output, failure and abstention rules | The task follows from the claim without broadening it |
| **Evaluate** | Observations, rival predictions, uncertainty and a recorded disposition | The evidence supports the exact conclusion proposed |
| **Review and revise** | Human fidelity review, correction history, AI-use disclosure | An accountable reviewer accepts the current content and limits |

This adapts the [Applied AI Research Translator](audit/APPLIED-TRANSLATOR-AUDIT.md). Reading packs expose the translation task and its limits. An application can receive **narrow**, **hold**, **reject**, or **abstain** as its proposed disposition. These are local analytical judgments; software cannot issue scholarly approval.

## Match the claim to the method

| Question | Method and required evidence | Permitted conclusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| What did the author argue? | Reconstruct premises, warrant, conclusion and scope from the inspected edition | Qualified interpretation, pending source-fidelity review |
| What was decided, and on what basis? | Dated instruments, packets, minutes and testimony | What the record establishes about the action and stated reasons |
| Did a mechanism operate? | Process tracing: condition → measurement → trigger → authorized response | Support for a specified mechanism, with missing links visible |
| Did the action change resource use? | Comparable observations, stable boundaries, plausible comparison, confounder assessment | Effect estimate only with defensible identification; otherwise descriptive association |
| Was the choice acceptable? | Explicit values, distribution, feasible alternatives and ecological constraints | A normative assessment with visible value judgments |

The [worked design](cases/RESEARCH-DESIGN.md) specifies rival predictions and a current **hold** on outcome attribution. It is a prospective plan, not a preregistration or completed causal study.

## What the outside influences contribute

| Influence | Practice used here | Where it changes the work |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rational reasoning / LessWrong | State assumptions, a strong rival, and update conditions | Hypotheses `H-01` / `H-02`; assumption failure rules |
| The Decision Lab | Describe actors, constraints and observable behavior before proposing intervention | Actor/authority records and feasibility questions |
| METR | Define task and comparator; distinguish perceived usefulness from observed performance | [Evaluation protocol](governance/EVALUATION.md), including what is unmeasured |
| Digital Emissions / IRIS | Trace transformations; define facility, time and measurement boundaries | Measurements, unit checks and decision-time evidence |
| Node & Norm / GDI | Reconstruct decision rights, alternatives, uncertainty and correction | Decision objects and review authority |
| E5 | Build a bounded argument a reader can inspect | [Writing](standards/WRITING.md) and [Markdown](standards/MARKDOWN.md) standards |

These are selected practices, not interchangeable methods or institutional endorsements. The [influence register](standards/INFLUENCES.md) names sources and limits. The former “bridges” page is now a migration pointer.

The [Assignment 1 revision](assignments/assignment-01/REVISION-NOTES.md) proposes a commitment-assurance chain: justification, disclosure, monitoring, triggers, authority and remedy. It organizes evidence requests for one decision. Its links, sufficiency and explanatory value remain open to testing; it supplies no finding that an accountability gap exists. The author-supplied Perplexity critique that suggested this sequence is disclosed in the AI-use record.

| Gate | Reviewer must establish | Current status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G1 · Source** | Claim, qualification and locator match the supplied edition | Pending in both class packs |
| **G2 · Lecture** | Important treatment is covered; speaker and transcript uncertainties are resolved or bounded | Pending in both class packs |

Reviews bind to content digests. Changed sources or dependent artifacts require renewed review. [Automated checks](governance/EVALUATION.md) detect some inconsistent records and stale approvals; they cannot determine whether a passage supports a sentence.

## The standard of scholarship

Doctoral research habits show up in a defensible question, source criticism, explicit causal and normative assumptions, serious alternatives, reproducible transformations, and willingness to withdraw an unsupported conclusion. Record counts, polished pages and named influences cannot establish scholarly contribution.

The next threshold is concrete: complete source and lecture fidelity review, retrieve comparable decision records, document case selection, and test one bounded mechanism. [Build status](BUILD-STATUS.md) records what is ready and what prevents that threshold.

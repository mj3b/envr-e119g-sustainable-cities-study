# Public-sharing policy

## Authorization and limits

On October 9, 2026, the owner stated that the move from private to public was intentional. The owner also reported that course staff permit AI use with disclosure. The latter is recorded as an owner report, not as an independently inspected faculty statement or endorsement of this repository, method, case, or draft.

The public surface contains authored research notes, methods, bounded analytical claims, public-source links and metadata, original code, synthetic tests, and owner-authorized review drafts. Course permission to use AI does not itself establish permission to republish another person's work.

Raw readings, lecture recordings, transcripts, private correspondence, unpublished feedback, sensitive personal information, API keys, and access-controlled sources remain outside Git. Public accessibility of an official record does not imply that every embedded personal detail should be repeated. Prefer the official link and the minimum relevant locator.

## Publication review

For each proposed artifact, record ownership or license basis, sensitive-content review, disposition, and exact content hash. Archive complete research exports separately when publication clearance is unresolved. A metadata-only listing is labeled as such. Do not upload a compressed bundle to evade a content restriction.

The inherited repository was already public when this update began. The existing history-path check remains active. A complete semantic, confidentiality, and rights audit of every historical sentence has not been completed. This working checkpoint makes no certification about that history. A suspected disclosure requires a separate exposure assessment; ordinary file deletion does not erase Git history or external copies.

## Automated enforcement

[Publication configuration](publication-policy.json) records the intended visibility. `scripts/publication_check.py` rejects obvious credential material and certain raw-course paths in current tracked/worktree content; the existing privacy checker examines forbidden paths throughout reachable history. These are limited controls, not a legal-rights or confidentiality determination.

No blanket license is added for the corpus. Original-source rights and institutional identifiers retain their existing status. No Harvard, IDB, instructor, or provider endorsement is asserted.

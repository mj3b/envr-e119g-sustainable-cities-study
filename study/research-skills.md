The urban applications are our synthesis. The machine-learning examples do not establish an environmental research method’s validity. A visual pattern suggests a question; it does not establish a causal mechanism. Evaluation can expose limitations but cannot guarantee validity merely because a checklist was completed.

Before using a chart, record who collected the observations, the exact source location, what each axis measures, absolute versus intensity values, the baseline, missing values, and whether the data are measured or projected. Distinguish installed MW from consumed MWh. Any calculation must preserve inputs, formula, and uncertainty. Avoid charts assembled from incompatible facility boundaries or years.

Reflection should record an actual revision in thinking when one occurred. It may also record a belief that survived scrutiny, with the evidence and remaining limits. Do not manufacture a conversion story.

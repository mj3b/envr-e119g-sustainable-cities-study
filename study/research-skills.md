The screenshots' machine-learning examples do not validate an environmental application. A pattern in a chart can suggest a mechanism without establishing it.

## A chart note before a chart claim

| Check | Record before interpreting |
| :--- | :--- |
| Observations | Who collected the data, by what method, and where are the underlying records? |
| Axes and units | Power or energy? Withdrawal or consumption? Absolute quantity or intensity? |
| Boundary | Which facility, grid, watershed, residents, and operating period? |
| Comparison | Same definitions, measurement method, and baseline across observations? |
| Missingness | Missing months, excluded sites, reporting changes, or suppressed values? |
| Conclusion | Is the pattern descriptive, modeled, reported, or causally identified? |

Practice the distinction between capacity, intensity, totals, and effects in the [evidence laboratory](EVIDENCE-LAB.md). Use the [quantitative notes](../cases/QUANTITATIVE-NOTES.md) when a real calculation enters the case record.

## Reflection without a manufactured story

A useful entry records an actual research decision, a source that tested it, and the consequence for the inquiry. It may preserve uncertainty or a belief that survived review. No change of mind needs to be invented. The [Assignment 1 worksheet](../assignments/assignment-01/reflection-and-evidence-worksheet.md) leaves personal content for Mark to supply.

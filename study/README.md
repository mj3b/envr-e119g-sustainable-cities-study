## Choose a task

| Need | Open | Produce |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Test understanding without notes | [Retrieval questions](retrieval-questions.json) | An answer, a claim ID and locator, and one qualification |
| Practice evidence judgment | [Evidence laboratory](EVIDENCE-LAB.md) | A bounded answer, calculation, or justified decision to withhold |
| Prepare to discuss or defend an argument | [Discussion preparation](discussion-prep.md) | A claim, fair objection, reply, and unresolved question |
| Apply a research habit | [Research skills](research-skills.md) | A source appraisal, graph note, or explicit revision |
| Decide what to retrieve next | [Research agenda](RESEARCH-AGENDA.md) | A missing record and the conclusion it could change |
| Track actual learning | [Learning log](learning-log.md) | A student-authored attempt and evidence-based correction |

## A twenty-minute practice cycle

1. Choose one retrieval question and answer it from memory. Mark unsupported certainty.
2. Open its claim records and source locators. Compare the assigned reading with the lecture's use of it.
3. Complete one evidence-laboratory exercise. State why the available evidence permits or blocks the conclusion.
4. Record a correction or a surviving belief with its support. Set the next review after a related class or new source arrives.

[Course knowledge](../cross-course/README.md) supplies the arguments. [Methodology](../METHODOLOGY.md) explains the research design. [AI evaluation](../governance/EVALUATION.md) tests assistance and controls; it does not substitute for student understanding.

[Research value and originality](RESEARCH-POSITIONING.md) compares the inquiry with three nearby sources and separates a promising contribution from an unsupported novelty claim.

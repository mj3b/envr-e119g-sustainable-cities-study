# Evidence laboratory

[Home](../README.md) / [Study](README.md) / Evidence laboratory

**Purpose:** practice the distinctions that protect the research from persuasive but unsupported conclusions.

| Exercise status | Evidence treatment |
| :--- | :--- |
| Analyst-authored exercises and provisional answer keys; no student performance recorded | Real case statements link to existing claims. Synthetic examples are explicitly labeled and must not enter the empirical dataset |

Attempt each task before opening its answer. A satisfactory answer identifies what is known, what follows, what does not follow, and which next observation would matter.

## 1 · Attribute the argument

**Task:** a note combines “income does not settle social valuation” with “education and environmental conditions affect people's freedoms.” Can the whole note be attributed to the assigned Sen essay?

**Inspect:** `S-01`, `S-02`, and `L1-03` in the [claim registry](../cross-course/claims.json). Sen locators: PDF pp. 5–7, printed pp. 20–22. Lecture locator: `L1E`, 06:36–09:50.

<details>
<summary>Answer and limit</summary>

Retain two attributions. The assigned essay supports the recorded plural-evaluation argument; the instructor's capabilities framing has a separate lecture record. The combined urban application is our synthesis. A familiar concept associated with an author still needs support in the particular assigned text if that text is the citation.

**Withhold:** the assertion that the combined wording has been established in the assigned passage. Human fidelity review and transcript-role verification remain pending.

</details>

## 2 · Convert units without inventing consumption

**Task:** MLGW reports approval of an initial 150 MW supply request. What annual electricity consumption does this establish?

**Inspect:** `X-02`, `EXT-MLGW`, PDF pp. 1–2; [quantitative notes](../cases/QUANTITATIVE-NOTES.md).

<details>
<summary>Answer and calculation</summary>

It establishes no measured annual consumption. MW describes power. Annual electricity requires a time series or an explicit operating assumption.

For a **hypothetical non-leap year of continuous 150 MW draw**:

`150 MW × 8,760 hours = 1,314,000 MWh`

This is a scenario calculation. Actual draw, operating hours, curtailment, and the supply boundary remain unknown. Do not call the scenario a meter reading or an established maximum for every possible facility energy source.

**Next evidence:** interval grid-meter data and the period and facility boundary to which they apply.

</details>

## 3 · Test an efficiency headline

**Synthetic exercise:** energy per unit of work falls 20%; completed workload rises 60%. Assume unchanged definitions and no other energy loads. Does total modeled energy fall?

<details>
<summary>Answer and interpretation</summary>

No. Relative modeled energy is `0.80 × 1.60 = 1.28`, a 28% increase from the baseline. The efficiency improvement and total increase can both hold.

This demonstrates arithmetic under stated assumptions. It does not establish that an efficiency change caused workload growth, or that any real facility experienced these values. Link the reasoning to `CD-02` and `SYN-02`; keep the synthetic inputs out of case measurements.

**Next evidence for a real case:** compatible workload and energy observations, other loads, definitions, and operating periods.

</details>

## 4 · Reconstruct what was knowable

**Synthetic exercise:** a council votes on January 10. A study is published on January 14. A later official says staff had seen a draft. Can the final study be recorded as evidence available to the council at the vote?

<details>
<summary>Answer and record treatment</summary>

Withhold that classification. The public publication date follows the decision. The retrospective account introduces a lead about earlier access but does not identify the draft's content, recipients, receipt date, or relation to the final version.

Record the final study as later evidence. Record the staff-access assertion separately, with source and uncertainty. Seek a dated transmittal, meeting packet, version history, or other contemporaneous record linking a specific version to the relevant actor.

**Key distinction:** public availability, staff access, and council access are separate claims. Avoid inferring any of them solely from the event date printed on a report.

</details>

## 5 · Preserve a numerical disagreement

**Task:** The Dalles minutes use 3.8 and 3.9 million gallons per day in discussion of transferred water supply or rights. Which value should populate facility water consumption?

**Inspect:** `CASE-DAL-03`, `EXT-DAL-MIN`, pp. 4–5; [worked example](../cases/worked-example.md).

<details>
<summary>Answer and retrieval choice</summary>

Neither value establishes facility consumption. Preserve both reported figures with their passages and meanings. Averaging them would conceal the inconsistency and still would not produce a meter observation.

Possible explanations include different definitions, rounding, or transcription. These remain possibilities. Seek the executed agreement, rights instruments, referenced studies, and compatible meter records. A corrected figure may resolve the document inconsistency while leaving actual water use unknown.

**Withhold:** claims of a measured consumption level, depletion rate, or sufficient local supply.

</details>

## 6 · Separate a changed outcome from an effect

**Synthetic exercise:** average monthly facility electricity falls from 100 to 80 MWh after a reporting condition begins. The same months also have lower workload. What can the comparison establish?

<details>
<summary>Answer and design implication</summary>

If the observations have compatible meters, periods, and boundaries, they establish a 20% before/after decline. They do not establish that the reporting condition caused it. Workload is a plausible competing explanation; seasonal conditions or measurement changes may also matter.

Seek the mechanism: did reporting change behavior, trigger enforcement, or reveal a reduction that had already occurred? Compare workload-adjusted observations and, where feasible, an appropriate untreated comparison or credible policy discontinuity. If the available design cannot separate explanations, report a descriptive sequence and its limits.

**Withhold:** a quantified causal benefit attributed to the condition.

</details>

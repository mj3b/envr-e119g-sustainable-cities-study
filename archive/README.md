## What the archive does not contain

The [preservation index](../research/preservation-index.json) inventories 30 recoverable exports. Two designated source-closure exports are published in full. Other exports remain separately preserved pending item-level public-sharing review. An indexed filename establishes neither public availability nor direct inspection of an original scholarly or administrative source.

Raw course readings, recordings, transcripts and private correspondence remain outside this public archive. Course interpretation pages remain in the [course collection](../cross-course/README.md); older does not mean obsolete.

## Maintenance rule

A future physical move must record the old path, new path, reason and content hashes, update inbound links, preserve any necessary compatibility pointer, and rerun validation. Protected source-closure exports, canonical evidence records and human reviews require their existing integrity controls. Archive status changes navigation; it cannot turn a blocked claim into an accepted one.

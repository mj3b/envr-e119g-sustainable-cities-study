## Disclosure and references

Describe the actual extent and location of AI contributions: retrieval, translation, research design, methods, interpretation, drafting, critique, code, images, narration, and formatting where used. Substantive assistance must not be described merely as proofreading.

At the author's request, include entries for AI tools actually used in the assignment's APA-style References, alongside an AI Use Disclosure explaining their contribution. Confirm final citation formatting against applicable course and citation guidance. Record verified provider, tool or model, version when available, year, and suitable public URL. Do not fabricate missing metadata or publish private conversation links. References to AI document assistance; substantive claims still require original readings and case records.

Maintain a concise disclosure in the deliverable and a fuller contribution record identifying affected sections, tools, evidence access, checks, and limitations. A short acknowledgment does not replace documentation.

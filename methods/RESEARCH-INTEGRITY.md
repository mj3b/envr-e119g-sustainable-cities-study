# Research-integrity protocol

Status: prospective protocol for work after October 9, 2026. Earlier exploration remains retrospective. This is not a preregistration of the original case search or an institutional certification.

## Substantive method

The study uses a bounded documentary case analysis of an urban development decision. The unit is a dated claim and its treatment in a particular instrument. The method reconstructs the connection between evidence, institutional authority, and recorded action. It does not estimate an environmental treatment effect.

ALLEA's *European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity*, revised 2023, supplies the general integrity reference: reliability, honesty, respect, and accountability (section 1); documented procedures and disclosure of AI/automated tools (2.3); stewardship and proportionate access restrictions (2.5); transparent authorship and corrections (2.7). This repository operationalizes those principles; it does not claim ALLEA certification. [Official text](https://allea.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/European-Code-of-Conduct-Revised-Edition-2023.pdf).

## Claim-to-evidence procedure

1. Freeze the current question, case period, inclusion rules, rival explanations, and permitted contribution before the next analytical extension. Timestamp any subsequent change and explain it.
2. Register the original instrument or scholarly source, issuer/author, version, date, official locator, retrieval date, file hash when bytes are available, and exact inspection scope. Distinguish a missing record from a retrieval failure.
3. Extract a bounded claim. Record the passage and the inferential step separately. Classify whether support is direct, attributed through another record, interpretive, contradictory, or insufficient. A judgment is primary evidence of its own holding, but secondary evidence of a filing it describes.
4. Check alternative readings and contrary evidence. Model criticism may propose objections; the source record decides whether an objection is substantiated. Two models reading the same evidence are not two independent sources.
5. Check dates, values, units, denominators, time bases, attribution, and reference coverage. A water right, equipment capacity, model input, peak design demand, forecast, permit limit, and observation require different labels. No annualization without supported operating assumptions.
6. Record an AI/source check and a human review separately. Human acceptance records reviewer, date, exact content version, retained scope, reasons, and unresolved issues. A high model score cannot fill a missing source field or sign a review.
7. When support changes, reopen the claim and downstream conclusions, preserve the old record, document the correction, and repeat affected checks. Final course submission remains an explicit human action.

## Spanish-language legal material

Keep the Spanish locator authoritative. Any English translation or paraphrase is a separate derived artifact with its tool, prompt availability, version, and reviewer status. Review negatives, modality, actor, scope, and temporal qualifiers. Preserve terms such as *consulta de pertinencia*, RCA, and *renuncia* where an English label could hide legal differences. Back-translation or agreement between models is a diagnostic, not certified bilingual legal review. Flag consequential ambiguity for a qualified reader.

## Review gates

An automated gate can establish that fields exist or hashes match. An AI review can identify a candidate inconsistency. Scholarly judgment establishes whether evidence actually supports the sentence. Publication review determines what may be shared. These gates remain separate.

A draft may present supported findings and open questions with visible limits. Submission requires the current assignment prompt, reference completeness, review of every material claim against its cited version, approval of personal voice, and an accurate disclosure. No optional evaluator is a prerequisite for writing the narrowed review draft.

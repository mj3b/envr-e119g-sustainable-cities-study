## Question

How did the Second Environmental Tribunal distinguish the legal effect of the 2022 consulta de pertinencia from RCA 127/2020 when it reviewed the Cerrillos Data Center's original groundwater assessment in 2024, and what legal consequences followed from that distinction?

## Contribution

This study reconstructs how an original environmental qualification, a later pertinencia screening, judicial review, and eventual renunciation operated as legally distinct stages in one urban data-center decision, examining why the 2022 screening did not displace judicial review of the original groundwater assessment.

This is the preserved source-closure contribution, not a claim that the complete underlying administrative file has been inspected. The draft attributes the court's account of missing records to the court.

# Cerrillos: the proposed redesign and the permission to proceed

[Home](../../README.md) / [Research](../README.md) / Cerrillos · [Read the draft](../../assignments/assignment-02/review-draft-v01.md)

This is a bounded documentary study of the Cerrillos Data Center proceedings in Chile, 2020–2024. The current object is how public instruments treated a proposed cooling redesign. Environmental outcomes remain outside the established evidence.

## The question

How did the Second Environmental Tribunal distinguish the legal effect of the 2022 consulta de pertinencia from RCA 127/2020 when it reviewed the Cerrillos Data Center's original groundwater assessment in 2024, and what legal consequences followed from that distinction?

A *consulta de pertinencia* asks whether a project or modification needs to enter environmental assessment. An RCA is the project's environmental qualification. The study examines their treatment in the court record; it does not offer current legal advice. [Source and locator register](draft-source-checks.json), `DRAFT-S01`–`DRAFT-S02`.

## Six claims that require different records

| Evidence stage | What a reader must establish |
| :--- | :--- |
| Proposed design | Which equipment and operating assumptions the proponent described |
| Projected resource effect | What demand the design predicts, in which units and conditions |
| Administrative screening | What entry-to-assessment question the authority answered |
| Legally operative authorization | Which instrument established or changed binding requirements |
| Implementation | What was actually installed, operated or enforced |
| Measured environmental outcome | What comparable observations show, with attribution limits |

This is a classification for reading records, not a verified sequence of implementation. A record at one stage cannot supply evidence for every other stage.

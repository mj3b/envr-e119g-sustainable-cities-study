# Cerrillos source-closure sprint

The sprint closes enough of the **legal and institutional record** to keep Cerrillos, but it does not close the evidence needed for the approved technical question. The original 2022 redesign submission, its principal engineering attachment, the complete Resolution 202213101388, and Resolution 463/2021 could not be directly inspected. That prevents a defensible before/after technical comparison.

It does **not** prevent a narrower inquiry into how the 2022 *consulta de pertinencia* and RCA 127/2020 were treated as legally different instruments during the 2024 judicial review. The official RCA, SEA's contemporaneous instruction governing *consultas de pertinencia*, the official Tribunal judgment, and the 2024 renunciation resolution are directly accessible and sufficient for that bounded question.

This keeps the earlier evidentiary rule intact: institutional process, implementation, and environmental outcomes remain separate; AI receives no special role without case-specific evidence.

## 5. Closed claims

The following claims are supportable now:

1. **RCA 127/2020 approved a water-cooled data-center configuration** that used cooling towers and treated 169 L/s as a central project water quantity. The supporting project documents give that number multiple precise roles, including peak/worst-case modeling and a restrictive constant pumping assumption. It is not a measured annual withdrawal. ([firma.sea.gob.cl](https://firma.sea.gob.cl/publicaciones/d4/3d/2145803977.pdf))

2. **The project held water rights exceeding its modeled 169 L/s demand assumption.** The Adenda identifies 228 L/s in project-associated rights and 169 L/s as modeled worst-case consumption. These numbers cannot be substituted for each other. ([seia.sea.gob.cl](https://seia.sea.gob.cl/archivos/2019/10/30/ADENDA_1_30102019.pdf))

3. **The original RCA already contained adaptive groundwater controls.** Monthly monitoring, escalation when drawdown exceeded modeling, immediate corrective action, higher-frequency monitoring, and possible adoption of less water-intensive technology or another water source were specified. A claim that the original authorization contained no response mechanism would be false. ([firma.sea.gob.cl](https://firma.sea.gob.cl/publicaciones/d4/3d/2145803977.pdf))

4. **A consulta de pertinencia had a legally narrower function than an RCA under SEA's contemporaneous instruction.** It supplied an opinion on mandatory SEIA entry; it did not amend the RCA or itself authorize execution. ([sea.gob.cl](https://www.sea.gob.cl/sites/default/files/imce/archivos/2022/06/06/of_202299102452.pdf))

5. **The Tribunal itself treated the 2022 pertinencia and the original environmental qualification differently.** Its 2024 reasoning says the pertinencia was not binding on the environmental evaluation and therefore did not extinguish review of the original groundwater assessment. ([tribunalambiental.cl](https://tribunalambiental.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2024.02.26_Sentencia_R-271_270-2020.pdf))

6. **The court partially annulled and suspended the original authorization pathway.** It ordered renewed consideration of the water component and required a complementary RCA before execution. ([tribunalambiental.cl](https://tribunalambiental.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2024.02.26_Sentencia_R-271_270-2020.pdf))

7. **Dataluna later renounced RCA 127/2020.** SEA's 2024 resolution accepted that renunciation and declared that the project could no longer be executed under those RCA terms. ([seia.sea.gob.cl](https://seia.sea.gob.cl/archivos/2024/09/12/Res._N_202413001362_firmada.pdf))

8. **Withdrawal from litigation cannot be used as evidence of representative community agreement.** The official Tribunal page records withdrawals and gives an institutional account of the reasons, but the underlying participant filings remain uninspected and the withdrawals represent particular parties, not a population survey. ([tribunalambiental.cl](https://tribunalambiental.cl/audiencia-r-271-270-2020-cerrillos-data-center/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## 8. Exact research question permitted by the evidence

The approved full technical question is **not yet supportable** because its first clause requires direct comparison of the 2022 redesign with the original groundwater assumptions.

The strongest question the closed evidence currently permits is:

> **How did the Second Environmental Tribunal distinguish the legal effect of the 2022 consulta de pertinencia from RCA 127/2020 when it reviewed the Cerrillos Data Center's original groundwater assessment in 2024, and what legal consequences followed from that distinction?**

This is narrower than the conditionally approved question. It asks about **institutional treatment**, not the unverified engineering magnitude of the redesign.

It still follows Assignment 1's stated research trajectory: reconstruct the evidence and assumptions, examine how institutional mechanisms connect evidence to action, and keep observed environmental change separate from causal claims.

## 9. Exact contribution permitted by the evidence

> **This study reconstructs how an original environmental qualification, a later pertinencia screening, judicial review, and eventual renunciation operated as legally distinct stages in one urban data-center decision, examining why the 2022 screening did not displace judicial review of the original groundwater assessment.**

This is an empirical reconstruction. It makes no novelty claim.

The useful feature of Cerrillos at this stage is concrete: SEA's own instruction distinguishes screening from authorization, and the Tribunal later applied that distinction when deciding that the original water issue remained reviewable. ([sea.gob.cl](https://www.sea.gob.cl/sites/default/files/imce/archivos/2022/06/06/of_202299102452.pdf))

## 10. Exact non-claims

## Minimum intellectual structure

| Role | Reading | What it changes in the memo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Institutional explanation | North, supplied Eafit reproduction | Separate formal rules, responsible actors and enforcement characteristics. Do not infer behavior from wording alone. |
| Sustainable-cities criterion | Satterthwaite, supplied 1997 article | Explain why resource decisions concern people, ecosystems and costs across boundaries and time. |
| Document-analysis method | Lowe and colleagues, 2022 | Separate policy presence, specificity and measurable targets from implementation. Add legal effect as a separate case-specific field. |
| Inference discipline | Blackman and Hoffmann, 2025 | Separate an intervention and hypothesized mechanism from reported response and modeled consequence. |
| Ecological qualification | Costanza and Daly, 1992 | Distinguish project-demand flows from aquifer stocks and ecological sufficiency. Use a short targeted discussion. |

North and Satterthwaite remain the theoretical center. Lowe is the strongest addition to the document-analysis method. Blackman and Hoffmann can justify an inferential caution without adding an app case or its numerical benefits to the memo. Costanza and Daly sharpen the sustainability boundary; they do not supply the missing water assessment.

Sen becomes useful when the memo explicitly examines evaluation criteria or whose concerns enter deliberation. Ostrom becomes useful when case evidence supports a cooperation, trust or monitoring mechanism. Without that work, either citation would add a name rather than an explanation. The other Module II readings remain in the study dossier as distributional, measurement and policy-comparison resources.

## Revision map for the existing draft

**Opening and question.** Retain the bounded distinction between the 2022 screening and review of the original authorization. Explain why that distinction matters to a city confronting a resource-intensive development. Avoid reopening a general theory of urban corrigibility or an AI-specific causal argument.

**Course foundation.** Replace any reliance on an abstract-only or secondary account with a precisely located passage from the now-accessible supplied article where it supports the same claim. Preserve a separately inspected Nobel or AER source as a separate version. A restored Eafit file does not validate an AER page citation, and a restored 1997 Satterthwaite article does not validate a 2021 chapter citation.

**Method.** Borrow Lowe's discipline of systematic content coding while stating the local adaptation. For each original instrument, record the issuer, date, function, relevant claim, specification, quantitative target if present, qualification, operative wording and evidence of implementation. Keep absence of a target distinct from absence of a policy, and both distinct from failure of implementation. Lowe's published study did not test implementation or government tracking, so it cannot certify this adapted legal analysis.

**Mechanism.** State what connects a technical proposal to a legal consequence only when the record supports that connection. The proposed chain should remain decomposed: design description, projected demand, screening, environmental qualification or other authorization, implementation and measured environmental change. The course comparison explains why those links require separate evidence; it does not supply them.

**Rival explanations.** Keep adequate regulation and ordinary screening, deficient initial assessment, weak incorporation of redesign into authorization, independent technical/commercial redesign, and unequal access or capacity as rivals. Ask what original evidence would favor or weaken each. Do not use a paper about another country to assign one explanation to Cerrillos.

**Quantitative reasoning.** Use quantities where they discriminate between interpretations. For a future technical comparison, the denominator must be compatible service or thermal demand under stated operating assumptions. A rights volume, design peak and modeled annual use are different quantity types. No annualization, saving percentage or aquifer-recovery estimate is presently justified by the missing design records. Simple descriptive counts in the research repository measure recordkeeping, not environmental success.

**AI disclosure.** State that AI assisted source reading, candidate extraction, comparison and revision planning; Python hashed source files and serialized records; pinned upstream Translator scripts validated candidate artifact structures. No live provider-backed Translator runloop, Jev evaluation, human gate, scientific replication or course submission occurred in this exercise. Mark must perform and record the final scholarly review.

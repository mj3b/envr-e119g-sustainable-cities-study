## Source selection

The minimum intellectual structure remains North for institutions, Satterthwaite for sustainable-development boundaries, and Lowe for systematic policy-content analysis. The Amazonia volume can supply a short addition on ecological fit and an explicitly located limit on evaluating promising interventions. Costanza and Daly remain useful where the stock/flow distinction is required. Blackman and Hoffmann remain an inference comparison rather than another empirical case.

The other Module III readings can stay in the research dossier. Their inclusion in a documented review does not create a duty to cite them in the memo. Every additional citation should improve an identifiable sentence, coding decision, comparison or limitation.

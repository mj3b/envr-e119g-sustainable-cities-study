# Select a decision that can be investigated

The case family is fixed; geography is open. Compare candidates on a documented decision, bounded place/time, available records, observable resource consequences, affected people, and Mark’s ability to evaluate the evidence. Do not score unknown evidence as zero impact.

Two researched leads are in candidate-family.json. Loudoun offers an institutional-rule decision; Memphis offers a facility-specific electricity decision. Neither has a complete decision/outcome packet here. Each needs contemporaneous primary records and affected-community evidence before selection. Selection must record why alternatives were declined, data gaps, and the user’s personal connection.

The measurement register must distinguish capacity (MW) from energy (MWh), water withdrawal from consumption, direct from upstream water, absolute from intensity metrics, operational from embodied impacts, location-based from market-based emissions, and projected from observed outcomes. Record units, boundary, baseline, geography, observation period, method, uncertainty, and independence for every value.

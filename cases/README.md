## What counts as progress

A candidate advances when a consequential gap is resolved: an operative decision is found, evidence access at the time is established, a population boundary is specified, or a measurement can be checked. An additional article repeating an existing release does not create another independent observation.

Primary records are the evidentiary backbone. Journalism supports discovery and chronology; interested-party statements support claims about what those parties represented. Community testimony can establish an expressed concern or lived account without establishing a population-wide causal effect. Hugging Face may help locate or later distribute a permitted derived dataset; it is not the default authority for urban decisions.

The [candidate family](candidate-family.json), [discovery assessments](discovery.json), and [research objects](research-objects.json) are canonical structured records. The original decision and measurement templates remain compatibility sketches; new populated work follows the typed research-object contract. No case has passed selection.

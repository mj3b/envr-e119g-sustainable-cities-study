Locators: vote p. 6; discussion pp. 4–5; attached public comment p. 7 (`CASE-DAL-01`–`CASE-DAL-03`, `EXT-DAL-MIN`).

The minutes contain both 3.8 and 3.9 million gallons/day. These unresolved supply or rights figures are not observations of facility consumption.

**Research question:** Which assumptions supported the supply assurance, who could inspect them before authorization, and what subsequent observations could test them?

## Interpretations to keep in contention

| Interpretation | Evidence that could distinguish it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Infrastructure investment improves supply sufficiently to accommodate additional demand | Executed terms, engineering and hydrology studies, implementation dates, and comparable source and demand observations |
| Additional demand creates pressure that assurances or aggregate supply figures obscure | The same observations, including seasonal conditions, other users' demand, uncertainty, and the relevant stock response |

Weather, other withdrawals, operating demand, and new infrastructure can change together. A before-and-after volume difference cannot isolate the agreement's effect.

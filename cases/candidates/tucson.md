**Research question:** How did access to information, binding commitments, and decision authority differ across the two pathways?

## Interpretations to keep in contention

| Interpretation | Evidence that could distinguish it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Disclosure constraints reduced meaningful scrutiny | Dated releases, restrictions, hearing submissions, decision timing, and records showing whether objections changed terms |
| Jurisdiction and existing commitments explain divergent decisions even with similar information | The same timeline plus authority provisions, contracts, alternative service arrangements, and stated reasons for each motion |

These explanations can coexist. Establishing restricted disclosure alone does not show it caused a particular decision.

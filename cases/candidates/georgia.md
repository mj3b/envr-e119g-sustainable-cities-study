## What the record supports

The [PSC announcement](https://psc.ga.gov/site/assets/files/8617/media_advisory_data_centers_rule_1-23-2025.pdf), p. 1, describes additional contract and billing terms for new customers above a stated 100 MW threshold (`CASE-GA-01`, `EXT-GA-PSC`). The adopted order is needed to establish exact applicability. The threshold is power; it is neither consumed energy nor measured cost protection.

**Research question:** How did the operative terms allocate demand-forecast and infrastructure risk, and which customers could bear residual costs?

## Interpretations to keep in contention

| Interpretation | Evidence that could distinguish it |
| :--- | :--- |
| New load spreads fixed costs and contract commitments protect other customers | Cost-of-service filings, realized load, customer payments, and the rule's treatment of underused infrastructure |
| Forecast error or incomplete cost recovery shifts burdens to other customers | The same records, including cancellations, exceptions, recovery periods, and alternative demand forecasts |

These are hypotheses. A later bill increase would not alone identify this rule's effect; fuel, other investments, usage, and rate changes must be separated.

## Interpretations to keep in contention

| Interpretation | Evidence that could distinguish it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Discretionary review improves siting and makes conditions more responsive | Before-and-after application rules, resulting conditions, documented reasons, and implementation records |
| The rule chiefly changes which projects apply or proceed, while regional demand follows other drivers | Application cohorts, withdrawals and approvals, project characteristics, timing, and jurisdiction-specific constraints |

A change in the approved-project mix could reflect selection into review. It would not alone establish improved outcomes for otherwise comparable projects.

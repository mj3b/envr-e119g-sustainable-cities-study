## What the record supports

The [Senate committee report on SCR95 SD1](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/session2026/CommReports/SCR95_SD1_SSCR3480_.HTM) recommends a working group and describes possible reporting, water-assessment, and grid-planning requirements. The relevant locators are Purpose, amendments (2)(A)–(E), and the concluding recommendation (`CASE-HI-01`, `EXT-HI-SCR95`). This does not establish final adoption, an implemented requirement, a permitted facility, or operational impacts.

**Research question:** Which bounded local decision would let us examine how an institution anticipates data-center resource demand and revises its assumptions?

## Interpretations to keep in contention

| Interpretation | Evidence that could distinguish it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Early reporting requirements improve planning and expose avoidable resource risks | Dated assessments, revisions prompted by them, and the decision-maker's recorded use of the information |
| The proposed requirements add cost or duplicate existing review without improving the decision | Existing review duties, proponents' and critics' submissions, and evidence of changed or unchanged decisions |

These are prospective analytical alternatives, not a documented local dispute. First identify the project and authority that could make them testable.

## Population and distribution plan

| Pathway to examine | Population and denominator needed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Water or electricity service | Accounts and households in the actual service area; statewide population is not a substitute for the affected system. |
| Participation and valued uses | Identify relevant communities and rights-holders from the decision record and their own accounts. Count submissions only as submissions; do not infer the share of residents represented. |

Demographic analysis needs a dated, justified geographic boundary and uncertainty estimates. Community identity cannot be inferred from residence, surname, or the researcher's connection to the place.

**Advance when:** one local action, authority, resource boundary, and credible record path are established. **Hold when:** evidence remains at the statewide inquiry level. Unknown dimensions remain unknown, without a penalty score or invented impact estimate.

# Select a case the evidence can sustain

[Home](../README.md) / [Cases](README.md) / Selection protocol

The purpose of discovery is to choose one decision that can support a semester inquiry. Controversy, personal connection, and record volume can motivate investigation; each still requires an answerable question. This protocol is our research design, informed by `L2-05`–`L2-07`, `TA-01`, and `TA-04`, not an added course requirement.

## Define the eligible unit

Specify one actor’s consequential action, the affected urban system, an identifiable place, a decision date or bounded process, and an observation window. Record a rule change and a facility approval as different decision types. A rejected proposal may qualify for a study of decision process; it cannot provide operational measurements for a facility that never operated.

Hawaiʻi remains a discovery geography until a local action is identified. Georgia’s statewide rule needs an urban anchor. Tucson’s city and county pathways must be separated before choosing an anchor. Memphis carries no default preference.

## Inspect six dimensions

| Dimension | Evidence that advances the candidate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Primary-record richness | Opened instruments, agendas, studies, filings, testimony and datasets with locators |
| Decision traceability | Who decided, under what authority, on what date, with what conditions and alternatives |
| Quantitative evidence | Usable units, periods, boundaries, baselines, uncertainty and realistic access to observations |
| Affected populations | Defined population and pathways of benefit or burden, with participant voices and denominators |
| Competing interpretations | Strong rival explanation, disconfirming search, and independent support where possible |
| Course fit | Focused theme/place/time; relevant concepts; a case Mark can understand and revisit |

Scores describe the inspected evidence: **1** is a partial lead, **2** usable evidence with material gaps, and **3** triangulated evidence with boundaries and contrary interpretations examined. **0** requires a documented search showing no usable support within that scope. **Unknown** remains `null`. No total or weighted average is used; missing evidence cannot be compensated for by a large score elsewhere.

These ordinal judgments express research readiness. They do not measure a city’s sustainability, the severity of harm, or the merits of approval. The reasons and source IDs matter more than the numeral.

## Control the search

Complete one comparable source pass for all six candidates before deepening a favored case. Record queries, opened records, access failures, and untested leads in the [discovery log](../audit/SOURCE-DISCOVERY.md). Advance the two most answerable candidates plus a personally relevant unresolved candidate through one bounded retrieval pass. The current retrieval order is a work queue, not a winner.

After that pass, stop accumulating general background. Compare actual missing records and choose whether to select, narrow, or defer. Record why each alternative was declined. A discovery failure means “not established within this search,” never “no harm” or “no relevant records exist.”

## Selection requires a written decision

To mark a candidate ready, all six dimensions must reach at least 2 and a dated primary decision record must be present. A case with inaccessible outcome data may be narrowed to a descriptive decision-evidence question, with that limitation explicit; it cannot promise a causal effect estimate.

Before geography is locked, create `selection-decision.json` with the selected candidate, bounded question, decision date and authority, rationale, alternatives and reasons, source IDs in `evidence_ids`, acknowledged gaps, personal fit, reviewer, and review date. Synchronize candidate flags, family selection, and discovery selection. Validation requires consistency and rejects a selected case that has not passed discovery checks. Human judgment remains necessary; no script selects geography.

## Biases to test before selection

Ask whether record abundance favors well-resourced jurisdictions, whether controversy substitutes for a tractable question, whether familiarity is being mistaken for evidence, and whether selecting a realized harm would prejudge the outcome. Preserve a credible benign or successful-governance interpretation. Record what observation would weaken the preferred account.

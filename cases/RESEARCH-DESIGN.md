## The bounded task

For the November 8, 2021 authorization in The Dalles (`DEC-DAL`), reconstruct water-supply assurances and public requests for evidence in the council record. Ask what additional records would distinguish effective monitoring from a change caused by operations or weather.

This is a data-center governance precedent. The inspected record does not establish how much of the facility's workload served AI; application to AI infrastructure is a bounded analogy.

## Two explanations with distinguishable predictions

| Explanation | Support | Contrary evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **H-01:** enforceable monitoring contributes to correction | Named trigger; responsible actor receives measurement; dated response; observations change in a plausible interval | No monitoring; no response after trigger; improvement predates response |
| **H-02:** operations or external conditions explain change | Demand tracks workload, weather, cooling or reporting coverage without enforcement | Comparable operating conditions change specifically with an implemented response |

Both mechanisms may operate. Evidence against one does not automatically prove the other. An operator's statement may identify an event or hypothesis; independent records are needed where the conclusion depends on its accuracy.

## Specify success before examining a result

| Proposed inference | Minimum evidence | Withhold it when… |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Authorization occurred | Dated vote and instrument identity | Only a proposal or unsigned draft exists |
| A control operated | Condition, trigger, actor and response record | Only intended operation is described |
| Physical use changed | Comparable meters, units, periods and missingness; withdrawals, consumption and return flows distinguished | Rights, capacity or modeled values substitute for measured use |
| Governance caused change | Justified comparison and assessment of major rival explanations | Only temporal order or an uncontrolled before/after difference is available |

The minutes support the recorded authorization and competing positions. Implemented operation and physical consequences remain unresolved. This is a **narrow documentary result**, pending human review; the attribution task remains on **hold**.

## Comparability and uncertainty

| Risk | Test | Consequence if unresolved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Changing boundaries (`ASM-01`) | Match facility IDs, meters, other customers, periods and definitions | Split series; withdraw combined trend |
| Hindsight (`ASM-02`) | Separate event, publication and actor-access dates | Treat later evidence as retrospective |
| 3.8 / 3.9 million gallons/day (`CON-DAL-01`) | Inspect executed transfer instruments and definitions | Preserve both reported figures; neither is metered use |
| Missing population denominator | Define service area, users and exposure pathway | Withhold community-wide benefit or burden claim |
| One interested account | Seek underlying instrument and independent observation | Attribute the statement and narrow confidence |

If quantitative analysis becomes feasible, record comparison choice, excluded periods, missing-data handling and sensitivity checks before estimating an effect. Explain changes made after viewing results. The current packet justifies no statistical test or sample-size claim.

## Values require an additional argument

Sen helps ask whose opportunities and participation matter. Costanza and Daly help identify ecological stocks or constraints an aggregate benefit could conceal. Neither supplies a numerical social welfare weight. A normative assessment must identify affected groups, feasible alternatives, distribution, and the value judgment used to compare them.
